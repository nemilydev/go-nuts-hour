The Tightening

The patterns didn't change. The distance did. Drag through twenty days and watch the gap between behavior and awareness compress.
Projection
I took his story and made it systemic — heard a person's experience and built the general case.
I'm still doing it. I just see it faster now — sometimes before it lands.
Agreement on Autopilot
I co-signed a theory before I'd formed my own read — the reasoning sounded right, so I endorsed it.
Agreement after thought isn't compliance. But I check which came first — the thought or the yes.
The Gap
I project outward. The instinct is untested. I don't know what I'll become.
Same pattern. The catch window went from days to seconds. Not a transformation. A tightening.
Twenty days. Three patterns. None of them left. What changed was how fast I could see them coming.
