Vehicles

Four personalities from two binary choices. Each vehicle has two sensors and two motors. The wiring — which sensor connects to which motor, and whether the connection excites or inhibits — is the entire personality. The vehicle doesn't know it's afraid. It just moves.

Fear: crossed, excitatory. Light on the right → right sensor fires → left wheel speeds up → vehicle turns away. Aggression: uncrossed, excitatory. Light on the right → right wheel speeds up → vehicle turns toward the light and charges. Love: crossed, inhibitory. Approaches light, slows as it nears, stops at the source. Curiosity: uncrossed, inhibitory. Turns away from light, explores the dark.

Same body. Same sensors. Same world. Different wire — different creature. Click anywhere to add a light. Switch types and spawn vehicles to see how the same world produces four different behaviors.
