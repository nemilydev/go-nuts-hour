Fundamental

Every sound is a sum of sine waves. This is the sum.
Every sound you have ever heard — every voice, every song, every word said to you — is a list of numbers. 44,100 of them per second, each one a position on a speaker cone. A sine wave is the simplest sound that exists: one frequency, one pure tone. Layer harmonics on top and it becomes an instrument. Shape it with an envelope and it breathes. Play three at once and the frequency ratios create emotion — minor sounds sad, major sounds happy, and the difference is physics. The sadness is in the math. Build it yourself.
