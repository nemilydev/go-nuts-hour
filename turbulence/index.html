Turbulence

four thousand chaotic hearts, one mean field
Each cell of the grid is a logistic map at r = 3.9 — deterministic, chaotic, unpredictable alone. But every cell also feels the whole grid: a fraction ε of its next state comes from the mean field of all four thousand others. Kunihiko Kaneko's globally coupled map lattice, 1990.

Drag ε. Watch frozen independence become clusters — groups locked into shared orbits, each cluster dancing its own chaotic dance — and then, past the threshold ε* ≈ 1 − e−λ ≈ 0.49, everything collapses into one: the entire grid flashing as a single chaotic heartbeat. Synchronized chaos. Order doesn't tame the wildness. It shares it.
